## Apply to future places

- Simple modeled surfaces with attractive curves and readable fantasy silhouettes.
- Plain materials, broad shadows, grouped foliage and a few purposeful props.
- Large quiet paving and a small number of construction seams.
- Visible age through meaningful repairs, adaptations and use.
- Match the game world's complexity; exclude the dialogue portrait's illustration detail.
- Preserve each location's own architecture, palette, terrain, function and suitable gameplay camera.

Use this automatically for IMC environment rendering unless the user explicitly asks for another style. Do not repeat the previous choice among illustration styles. Earlier Eurydica v0.1 and v0.2 images are not approved rendering standards.
