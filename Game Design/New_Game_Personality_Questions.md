# New Game Personality Questions

## Purpose

These questions are presented at the start of a new game as **flavor-only character-definition prompts**.

- They do **not** affect gameplay, stats, relationships, events, or story outcomes.
- Their purpose is to help the player define and self-insert into their character.
- Each question has four answers with deliberately distinct attitudes or personality expressions.
- There is no correct answer.

---

## Question 1 — The Last Piece

**There is only one piece of your favorite food left,  
and someone else reaches for it at the same time.**

1. Let them have it.
2. Grab it first.
3. Split it in half.
4. Challenge them for it.

---

## Question 2 — Unexpected Money

**You are given enough money to live comfortably for a year.  
What is the first thing you do?**

1. Save almost all of it for the future.
2. Spend it on something I've always wanted.
3. Share some of it with people close to me.
4. Use it to make even more money.

---

## Question 3 — A Friend's Cooking

**A friend proudly cooks dinner for you.  
Unfortunately, it tastes terrible.**

1. Eat everything and tell them it was delicious.
2. Tell them exactly what went wrong.
3. Make jokes about it until we're both laughing.
4. Quietly avoid eating as much as possible.

---

## Question 4 — The Locked Door

**You discover a locked door with a sign saying:  
“ABSOLUTELY DO NOT ENTER.”**

1. Leave it alone. The sign is there for a reason.
2. Open it immediately.
3. Look around for someone who knows what's inside.
4. Wait until nobody is watching, then open it.

---

## Question 5 — Winning an Argument

**Halfway through an argument,  
you realize the other person is actually right.**

1. Admit I was wrong.
2. Keep arguing anyway.
3. Change the subject.
4. Pretend that was my point all along.

---

## Implementation Rule

These questions are **purely expressive**. Do not assign hidden personality scores, alignment values, stat modifiers, dialogue branches, relationship modifiers, story flags, or gameplay consequences to any answer.
