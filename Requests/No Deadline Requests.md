# No Deadline Requests

These requests have no countdown. The following three appear during Chapter 1 and can be offered before Scouting unlocks. Unaccepted offers disappear after Chapter 1; accepted requests remain completable afterward.

Rewards, material conditions, and exact inventory items are still to be finalized where marked TBD or pending.

## Winter Bedding

- ID: CH1-REQ-005.
- Location: Stables near the south gate, Eurydica.
- Appears: Chapter 1: at the end of Hollis's conversation, if Player offers to arrange the order.
- Client: Hollis.
- Reward: TBD.
- Time Limit: None.
- Request: x3 wolf pelts (exact inventory item pending).
- Client Description: Bedding for the person taking the overnight stable watch.
- Tristitia Text: "There is no deadline. We will need to scout Mosswood and locate wolves before arranging a hunt."

- Hunting requirement: Scout Mosswood to locate wolves and a hunting ground, then arrange hunts and processing.
- Repeatable: No.
