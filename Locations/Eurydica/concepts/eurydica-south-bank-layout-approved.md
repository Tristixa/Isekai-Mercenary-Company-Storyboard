## Purpose

Reconcile six approved district concepts into a single north-up relationship study. District geography follows the whole-city district map; junction positions are proposed, not measured. This is not a stitched scene, facade redesign, production footprint plan or scale verification. Colors encode planning, not materials. Original approved art remains authoritative for building design.

## Carried-forward decisions

- Main river to the north; Old Bridge is the only established crossing.
- Quays west of Market Spine; workshops southwest; Service Lanes east; Company Edge between Arrival Ward and market.
- Working courts and workshop-to-quay route use earth; Service Lanes streets and household courts are paved.
- Food shop, doctor's house/clinic and local connections are required Service Lanes functions.
- Warm mixed Service Lanes roofs and moderate trees; brown working-district roofs and sparse working-district planting. Other approved district palettes retained.

## Approved conceptual joins

1. Arrival branches west onto the earth working loop after entering the gate.
2. Quay route meets a market storage edge before the bridge approach; short paved apron at the join.
3. Market branches east into paved Service Lanes serving food and clinic.
4. Public Service Lanes loop returns to Arrival without routing traffic through the household court.
5. Company entrance branches off the principal street. Propose level/ramped delivery access; resolve existing concept steps when measuring terrain.
6. Direct workshop-to-market local supply branch joins south of the Service Lanes junction.

Point markers locate shared identities, not exact footprints. The original green roofs for the Arrival lodging house and South Gate are the approved shared-building direction. Their brown appearance in the Workshop image still needs a later targeted artwork reconciliation; this approval does not itself modify either district image.
