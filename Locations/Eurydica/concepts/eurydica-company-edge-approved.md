## District choice and continuity

Company Edge is the approved neighbor between Market Spine and Arrival Ward. It is a useful next scene because it continues the Chapter 1 arrival route and places the first Company property near the tavern and supply businesses.

The view overlaps the approved market: the tavern, copper tree, provisions awning and repair shop identify the upper part of the scene. The road continues south toward Arrival Ward. This is a conceptual continuity check, not a registered stitched extension or measured parcel map.

## Proposed new design

A modest adapted house with one main storey and loft uses the same warm plaster, substantial timber and curved roof construction. Its plum roof and green porch distinguish it from the green-roofed market businesses. A usable yard and small store shed communicate room to expand. The front gateway connects to the main street; an additional raised side path reaches toward the market. Sparse storage, a workbench and repaired shed roofing indicate daily use.

The image labels Market Spine, Tavern, Company House, Expansion Yard and the direction to Arrival Ward. Arrival Ward itself has not been designed in this image.
