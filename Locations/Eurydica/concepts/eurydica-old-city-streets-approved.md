## Approved design

Eurydica's first settlement, historic and maintained rather than pristine or abandoned. A modest group of south-facing timber homes and shops nestles into the rear cliff. The mostly timber Old Hall Inn is the original communal hall adapted into an inhabited, maintained inn; its name remains a working descriptive label.

A small public park with benches replaces the sheep meadow. The detached cottage is removed. A raised timber viewing terrace integrates with the inherited watch-bell tower and river overlook. A clearly separated natural-stone walk connects Civic Terrace, the inn, park and viewing terrace. Existing lamps guide visitors to the view Mae recommends.

This approval supersedes earlier dense urban blocks and the rejected sheep-meadow/barn-like inn concept. No royal or castle history is established. Preserve the approved zoomed-out camera and simplified modeled game appearance.
