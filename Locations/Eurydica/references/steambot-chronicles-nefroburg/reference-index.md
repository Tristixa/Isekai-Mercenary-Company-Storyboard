## Interpretation

The transferable quality is not Steambot's historical period. It is the way the city visibly connects geography, infrastructure, institutions, businesses, homes and everyday maintenance.

For Eurydica:

- Nefroburg is the target order of magnitude for the complete fictional city.
- References inform world design, scale, ownership and lived-in construction.
- They do not select IMC's gameplay camera or final rendering family.
- Eurydica must remain an original other-world fantasy city rather than a copy of Nefroburg.
