# Officer work animations (4-second loops), ready to paste

Written 2026-09-30, for **all seven officers**, including Minerva Alraun, the Frontier's Chief of Research. Each loop is what the officer does at their department station in the HQ. It follows the HQ-staff work-loop format in `Gemini Sprite Prompts - Cast v2.md` §5, and uses the shared rules in `Gemini Sprite Prompts.md`.

## How to make each one

- **Tool:** Omniflash, one video at a time, with the **Start frame** option (not Ingredients; Ingredients redesigns the character).
- **Start frame:** `Character Sprites/Officers/<Officer>/Cropped/Start - Facing Down.png`: row 1, facing down, the cleaned sprite at ×8 on a magenta 1920×1080 frame.
- **Key colour:** pure magenta `#FF00FF` for every officer, the same as their sheets and walks.
- **Save as** `Character Sprites/Officers/<Officer>/Work Loop.mp4`. The sprite pipeline cuts one clean loop, keys the magenta and snaps to the grid.
- **Stations are placed in the game.** No desk, counter, table or anvil appears in the video, so the loop works at any station.
- **Review:** it loops cleanly (the last frame matches the first), there's no mouth or nose, the outfit is unchanged, there are no effects, and the character stays centred.
