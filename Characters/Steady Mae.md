# Steady Mae

The name is a nickname rather than a formal title. People began calling Mae “Steady” because she does not rush, panic, or lose her sense of proportion. Eventually the nickname became more familiar than whatever formal name she may once have used.

## Character authority

This sheet is the authority for Mae's characterization. Draft dialogue and scene notes should be revised to fit this sheet rather than being allowed to redefine her accidentally.

## Role

- Supporting character and one of the Company's founding officers.
- Chapter 1: Former adventurer who processes the results of Tristitia's hunts and helps find buyers for the materials.
- Chapter 2 onward: Chief of Processing.

Mae is the Company's practical link between field work and useful results. She understands that an expedition is not finished when a monster stops moving; it is finished when the materials are processed, recorded, sold, stored, or put to work.

## Physical Description

- Age: Mid 20s.
- Height: Approximately 168 cm.
- Build: Athletic.
- Distinguishing features: Beauty mark, tattoo on her left hand, and a beautiful mature face.

## Core Identity

Mae is a veteran navigator and former adventurer with extensive experience on dangerous roads and in uncertain places. She has spent enough time around danger that it no longer automatically produces drama from her.

She is low-energy, experienced, unhurried, and quietly competent. She is not lazy, careless, or unintelligent. She simply does not spend ten units of emotional energy on a problem that requires two.

Her worldview is:

> Most problems become worse when people spend more urgency than the situation requires.

Her central contradiction is:

> She appears unhurried because she is very good at judging what truly deserves urgency.

## Personality

### Surface traits

- Relaxed and almost casual.
- Matter-of-fact about danger.
- Patient with uncertainty.
- Mildly amused by other people's panic.
- Comfortable letting silence continue.
- Warm through practical attention rather than emotional speeches.

### Strengths

- Quietly competent and reliable.
- Strong practical judgment.
- Experienced navigator and route planner.
- Understands processing, materials, storage, and trade.
- Good at noticing consequences before others do.
- Can de-escalate a situation by refusing to feed its panic.

### Weaknesses

- Her unhurried manner can become delay when immediate action is needed.
- She sometimes understates danger until other people realize how serious it is.
- She keeps her own history private and rarely volunteers explanations.
- Her calm can look like indifference to people who do not know her.
- She may continue carrying responsibility quietly instead of asking for help.

## Background

Mae used to travel and work as an active adventurer. The exact reason she stopped should remain unknown for the player to wonder about. Do not lock the explanation to an injury, failed expedition, personal tragedy, or single incident unless a later story deliberately establishes it.

What is established is that she now processes Tristitia's hunts, handles materials, and searches for buyers. She has chosen a role that stays closer to the practical aftermath of dangerous work, though the story should not assume that this choice was simple or emotionally effortless.

## Goals and Motivation

### Immediate goal

Keep work useful, sustainable, and organized. Materials should not be wasted, and people should not be sent into danger without preparation.

### Long-term direction

See whether the proposed Company can give adventurers a steadier structure than the unorganized work she left behind.

### Emotional need

Allow herself to care openly about the people and work she has chosen instead of hiding that care behind routine and understatement.

## Humor and Voice

Mae's humor is not deliberate comedy. She describes situations with such calm, practical accuracy that the result becomes funny.

- Use short, plain sentences.
- Let pauses and timing do part of the work.
- State the obvious consequence other people are avoiding.
- Underreact to danger without denying that it is dangerous.
- Use concrete work language: time, distance, weight, materials, food, repairs, and routes.
- Do not turn her into a constant quip machine.
- Her casual manner should feel like experienced judgment, not a cowboy caricature.

Preferred example:

> “I used to travel. Then I discovered that monsters are easier to deal with after they stop moving.”

Another example:

Adventurer: “There's something moving in the ruins.”

Mae: “Mm.”

Adventurer: “That's all you have to say?”

Mae: “Could be worse.”

Adventurer: “How?”

Mae: “Could be moving toward us.”

The joke works because Mae is not performing. She is calmly assessing the situation.

## Chapter 1 Behavior

- She greets Tristitia as a familiar working companion rather than making a dramatic reunion of it.
- She notices that Tristitia has brought someone new and lightly teases her about it.
- She gives the Player room to introduce themselves instead of immediately interrogating them.
- She explains the local adventuring situation in plain practical terms.
- When the Player describes an adventurer's company, her interest appears through questions about payment, supplies, records, processing, and what happens when someone does not return.
- Her eventual agreement should feel like a practical decision with emotional weight underneath it.

## Relationships

### Tristitia

Mae and Tristitia share long-earned, functional trust. Mae handles the aftermath of Tristitia's work, while Tristitia trusts Mae with the things she does not want to discuss.

Mae does not force Tristitia to explain her past or confess her feelings. She understands that practical care is the language Tristitia can accept. Mae may tease her, question her decisions, or quietly make sure she eats, but she does not treat Tristitia as a project to fix.

### The Player / Commander

Mae initially sees the Player as a stranger with an unusual idea and potentially useful resources. She becomes interested when the idea proves capable of solving real problems rather than merely sounding exciting.

She is more immediately approachable than Tristitia, but that does not mean she gives trust away freely. The Player earns her confidence through consistency, practical follow-through, and respect for the work behind the Company's promises.

## Important Distinction From Tristitia

Tristitia's coolness comes from emotional restraint.

Mae's calm comes from experience and proportion.

Tristitia protects people by controlling danger.

Mae protects people by preparing for consequences.

Tristitia tends to cut a problem down to its decisive point.

Mae gives a problem enough time and space to reveal what it actually needs.

## Core Feeling

> “There is probably a way through this. Let us not make it worse.”
