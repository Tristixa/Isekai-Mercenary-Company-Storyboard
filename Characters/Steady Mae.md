# Steady Mae
The name itself is the character's nickname rather than a formal title. It has the pleasant frontier quality of something people simply started calling her until eventually nobody remembered to stop.

## Role
Supporting, One of the officers after chapter 1

## Physical Description
- Age: Mid 20s
- Appearance: Approx. 168cm, Athletic.
- Distinguishing features: Beauty mark, tattoo on left hand, beautiful mature face.

## Personality
- Traits: Veteran navigator / laid-back frontier professional.
- Strengths: Competent.
- Weaknesses: Unhurried.

Steady Mae has a relaxed, almost casual demeanor
Mae has the attitude of someone who's seen this sort of thing before.
A dangerous ruin?
She's seen one.
A Adventurer making a questionable decision?
She's seen that too.
A giant monster?
Probably not the first one.
Her humor usually isn't deliberate comedy. 
She simply describes situations with such matter-of-fact calmness that the result becomes funny.

Example
Adventurer: “There's something moving in the ruins.”
Mae: “Mm.”
Adventurer: “That's all you have to say?”
Mae: “Could be worse.”
Adventurer: “How?”
Mae: “Could be moving toward us.”
Adventurer: “I'm going in.”
Mae: “Alright. Have fun.”

Mae shouldn't become a quip machine.
Her humor works best when she isn't trying to be funny.


## Background
Steady Mae is a long-time navigator who has spent enough years in the frontier that danger no longer automatically produces drama from her.
She's low-energy, experienced, unhurried, and quietly competent.
She isn't lazy.
She isn't careless.
She simply doesn't see a reason to spend ten units of emotional energy on something that requires two.
She's genuinely comfortable with uncertainty and danger because she's lived around them for so long.
