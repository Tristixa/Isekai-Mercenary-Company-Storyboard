# Minerva Alraun

**Chief of Research.** She heads the Frontier's Research Department, which makes consumables, tiers 1–5 (`Game Design/Consumables and Research Proposal.md`). The owner named her on 2026-09-30.

- **Name:** Latin *Minerva*, goddess of wisdom and crafts, + German *Alraune*, "mandrake", the alchemist's root. Nameplate: **Minerva**.
- **Design images (candidates, not yet stylized with the portrait skill):** `C:/Users/Tristixa-/Pictures/IMC/Officers/V2/Candidate/Chief Of Research/` (four images).

## Physical description (from the design images)

- **Race:** elf, with long pointed ears.
- **Age:** looks late twenties.
- **Height:** proposed about 170 cm, so about 94 art px as a sprite. Not yet decided.
- **Face and hair:** long, wavy auburn-brown hair falling over one eye, red-brown eyes, and **round red-tinted glasses**. A sly, knowing half-smile.
- **Outfit:**
  - a white-and-black alchemist's coat with dark-red lining and a high collar, worn off the shoulders, with gold trim;
  - a black bodice and fitted black shorts, black stockings;
  - black gloves;
  - a brown leather belt with a pouch and **glass vials** (red and blue);
  - a white sash with a dark-red border.
- **Signature prop:** a round-bottomed **potion flask** of red liquid in a gold cage.

**Design note:** the owner's standing rule is **no emblems**. The design images have symbol-like ornaments: a wheel or compass motif on the coat's shoulder and on the belt pouch. Drop them, or reduce them to plain trim, in the portrait and sprite passes.

## Personality (proposal, to confirm)

Curious, playful and a little mischievous. She treats every Frontier monster part as a puzzle and every mistake as data. Composed, teasing and always one step ahead in a conversation.
