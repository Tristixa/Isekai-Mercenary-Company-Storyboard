## Notes

Archetype: The regal, sassy, cool-headed older-sister type
Tristitia is restrained. She can come across as cold or dismissive, but that doesn't mean she doesn't care.
Her support tends to be indirect. She respects the Adventurer's competence and autonomy rather than constantly encouraging them.

Example:
Adventurer: “That room looks heavily guarded. I'm going in.”

Tristitia: “Just go there if you think you can do it.”
If the Adventurer succeeds, she may reveal that she was more impressed than she initially let on.

Tristitia: “Impressive work as usual, Adventurer.”

Tristitia: “.....”
That brief silence is intentional. The player can interpret it however they want:
Was she about to say something else?
Was she embarrassed about praising them?
Was she simply gathering her thoughts?
Did she almost reveal how impressed she really was?
Core feeling
“I trust you to handle yourself.”
