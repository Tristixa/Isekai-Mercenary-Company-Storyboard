## Notes
Archetype: The smart but clumsy and unsure officer

Liliana is another early concept.
She's intelligent and probably capable at her actual profession, but socially and verbally awkward. 
She thinks through things too much and has trouble expressing what she means.
When the Comander proposes something questionable, she may initially agree, then immediately start over-explaining why it might technically be reasonable, then realize she's making things worse.

Example:
Adventurer: “I'm checking that room.”
Liliana: “Sure, if that's what you want. Surely nothing bad will happen.”
Liliana: “Wait... no... I-I didn't mean it sarcastically.”
Liliana: “The probability of you surviving is actually quite good given your previous performance, so even though from this distance the room appears to be an obvious trap with nothing particularly worth investigating—”
Liliana: “N-not that I mean your idea is bad! More that...”
Liliana: “I think I'll just stop talking.”

Her intelligence should be genuine. The joke isn't that she's dumb; it's that her brain works faster than her ability to communicate what's happening inside it.
