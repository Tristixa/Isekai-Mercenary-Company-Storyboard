# Chapter 1 dialogue review

Date: 2026-10-02. This review covers the finished Chapter 1 manuscript (Scenes 1–8), checked against the owner's intentions in `Character Appeal and Dialogue Study.md` and the character sheets. **Owner decision (2026-10-02):** suggestions 2, 3, 5–7, 9 and 10 are applied in the manuscript. Suggestion 8 (a choice after Valerie's wink) was tried and reverted by the owner: the Commander is deliberately dense, a common isekai-protagonist trope (see the picking-flowers beat and the Elsie/Mae talk in Scene 8), so he answers without a choice. Suggestion 1 is kept as written: Elsie's "I do declare" is a deliberate dialect slip from her western nation (now noted on her sheet). Suggestion 4 is kept as written: Mae drops her warning because she is the kind older sister, not selfish.

## Verdict

**Tristitia, Mae and Elsie** are on target in Scenes 1–6, and their voices are distinct:
- Tristitia: dry and precise.
- Mae: plain and warm, with "Mmm."
- Elsie: questions, affirmations and teasing.

**Fulker** reads well. **Liliana and Valerie** have the right shape in Scene 8, but a few lines are generic or repeat the same joke.

There's one real voice slip: Elsie's "I do declare".

## What's working (keep)

- **Tristitia's care beneath the sharpness:**
  - she comes back as promised, and says "Don't sell everything";
  - "She's the first person I trust";
  - in Scene 8, "That would be the Commander." She gives him the credit, which is the care the study wanted made visible.
  - Scene 6's double kick reads as play, especially with a Menace portrait.
- **Mae:** "Mm-hmm. That is usually how people begin." and the "picking flowers" beat.
- **Elsie:** her interview in Scene 3, which carries her whole philosophy ("A contract is work, not a chain"), and the look-behind tease in Scene 4.
- **Fulker:** "Huh!? I'm gonna be the chief?", "Can someone else train me?" / "NO." Earnest and funny, and a good contrast to her intense portrait.
- **Liliana's first exchange** (answer, then qualification, correction and embarrassment) matches her sheet exactly.
