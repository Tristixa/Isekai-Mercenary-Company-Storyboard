# Portrait expressions

Every officer and the Commander have the eight base expressions: **Base, Happy, Serious, Sad, Anger, Fear, Surprise, Laugh** (`Game Design/Scene Script Format.md` §2).

This file lists the **extra expressions** each character can use. **Status: proposed 2026-10-02, waiting for the owner's choice.** None is drawn yet.

## What an extra expression is

- It suits the character's personality and is **reusable** in many scenes, not tied to one line. The model is Tristitia's threatening smile: anger shown as a smile.
- Until it is drawn, a line tagged with it shows its **fallback** (one of the eight).
- It is drawn like the other expressions: from the character's approved Base, with the same pose and crop.

**Priority:** Liliana **Flustered** and Valerie **Wink** first: Scene 8 already uses them.

## Not proposed

- Dedicated Blush, Crying or Pout sets: the base Sad, Fear and Surprise cover them.
- Anything tied to one line, such as a "kicking under the table" face.
