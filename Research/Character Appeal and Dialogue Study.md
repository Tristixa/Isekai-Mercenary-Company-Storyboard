# IMC Character Appeal and Dialogue Study

Date: 2026-10-01. Based on all eight manuscript scenes and officer notes. Commander excluded. Findings and suggestions, not new canon.

## Core Finding

The six officers offer distinct kinds of closeness through competence, independence and personal attention. Tristitia, Mae and Elsie are well established; Fulker has a promising introduction. Scene 8 has no dialogue yet: Liliana's assessment comes from her sheet, Valerie's from the owner's intentions.

## Officer Appeal and Voice

| Officer | Main appeal | Current voice and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Tristitia | Guarded devotion; sharpness becomes familiar protection. | Controlled instructions, dry precision, silences; mostly Base/Serious. Returning as promised and protecting treasured belongings reveal care. Scenes 1-2. |
| Mae | Warm acceptance; the older-sister feeling of home. | Plain, unhurried "Mmm," personal greetings, "We will find one eventually." Often Happy/Base. Humor through experience and proportion. Scenes 2, 4, 6. |

| Fulker | Earnest fascination contrasting with her intense, dark portrait. | Colloquial "Um... Yeah," "Huh!?" and enthusiasm. Wants to craft; surprised by leadership. Mostly Laugh/Surprise in Scene 7. |
| Liliana | Thoughtful intelligence exposed through awkward delivery. | Sheet: answer, qualification, correction, over-explanation, embarrassment. Accuracy and consideration overwhelm her delivery. No manuscript dialogue yet. |
| Valerie | Poised attention; attraction with clear personal limits. | Proposed: observant questions, specific compliments, amused reversals. Scene 6 establishes negotiation skill and selectiveness, not her voice. |

Portrait patterns cover all branches. Tristitia's self-sacrifice and Elsie's concealed-emotion smiles remain intentions requiring scenes.

## Best Improvements

- **Tristitia:** Make care legible beneath sharpness. Scene 6's repeated intimidation can obscure it. Growing trust should also permit accepting help.
- **Mae:** Add personal welcome, pleasure in company and her own wants. Steadiness currently outweighs the intended soft embrace.
- **Elsie:** Add invitations and shared time after work. Preserve genuine happiness; let some smiles conceal worry through changed wording.
- **Fulker:** Show what captures her attention. Application confusion also permits a clumsy reading. Express care through individualized crafting.
- **Liliana:** Let accurate information change decisions. Vary communication difficulties and permit clear warnings; competence must matter beyond the joke.
- **Valerie:** Demonstrate negotiation judgment alongside charm, including adapting to someone unmoved by flirtation. Enjoying attention need not promise romance.

Mae welcomes; Elsie accompanies; Valerie draws out reactions. Fulker's attention is pulled toward craft; Liliana struggles to communicate everything she considered.

## Why the Writing Feels Adult

"Adult" describes relational maturity here, not sexual content or superior writing quality.

- Characters invite the player into lives already underway, with existing responsibilities and relationships.
- Affection appears through follow-through: returning, protecting belongings, offering support and respecting refusal.
- Emotional space matters: Mae recognizes Tristitia's discomfort without demanding confession; Elsie preserves autonomy while negotiating responsibility.
- Ordinary shared time builds intimacy. Chasm Elsie's bonding scene similarly moves from winding down together toward valuing companionship.

Recruitment and meeting scenes foreground professionalism. Leisure scenes could reveal more exuberance, silliness and impulsiveness within the existing cast.

## Should There Be a More Shonen-Style Character?

No archetype is required to complete the cast. Cheerfulness and prickly affection can be mature; Elsie and Tristitia already offer those pleasures. "Shonen-style" here means more overt, youthful emotional energy, not the entire genre.

Useful contrast would come from openly expressed wants: excitement over a first success, eagerness for recognition, impulsive volunteering or visible embarrassment. A junior adventurer could supply this; the owner's younger, inexperienced Anselm direction is one possibility.

The risk is emotional sameness if everyone handles vulnerability through restraint. Broaden how people show joy, disappointment and need. Preserve the mature foundation while allowing more exposed emotion and playfulness.

## Other Characters

Jeb offers family warmth; Hilde cooking pride and customer frustration; Gerd patient affection for impractical attachments. Merchant and doctor appearances are brief. Minerva's untested playful proposal could overlap Valerie: emphasize intellectual discovery if developed.
