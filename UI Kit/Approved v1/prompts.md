# Native artwork brief and decisions

No image-generation requests were submitted. This kit extends v5's existing native Canvas composition and original artwork, as requested.

Design brief: keep v5's parchment, Navy, subdued patterned card perimeters, soft stains and owner watermark. Identity pieces may carry painted texture and depth. Tier 2 reads as separate paper/stain/watermark/frame/content layers. Tier 3 is plain and icon first. Use Font C and GDD 2.5 tokens, with caps card headers. Never copy reference crests, icons or text.

Craft glyphs as small engraved seals using v5's clipped-corner tile and inset rule. Build each meaning from distinct silhouettes; redraw at 24/32/48 rather than merely shrinking a sheet. Enlarge rank letters so the letter dominates the badge at 24 px. Maintain seven rank letters F through S.

Paint the ledger with two paper blocks, stacked page edges, a shaded spine, restrained cover depth, perimeter ornament and a bookmark. Keep watermark, quill and brush selection separate. Hover is a 35% wash; selected is solid Blue glow with a Blue select hairline. Put the world on the left and book on the right.

Battle composition: four party cards down the right, front-idle busts and clockwise gold timer rings, opaque names/HP/statuses/row tags, orange 0–100 skill meter at the lower edge. Two enemy bars at top centre, log bottom left. Use approved environment layers and existing cleaned actor stills. No HUD screenshot as backdrop.

Dialogue composition: canonical Mae Happy line from Scene 2, waist-length Commander left and Mae right, inactive portrait dimmed to 55%, one-ended nameplate, 140 px content box and advance cue. Reuse approved portrait files without redrawing identity.

Review corrections: replaced the flattened clock treatment; excluded annotated/baked HUD backgrounds from the visible crops; removed monster chroma; corrected front bust framing; softened ledger brush edges; separated distinct control states; added dark keylines where diagonal or transparent backgrounds needed them; verified corners after stretching.
