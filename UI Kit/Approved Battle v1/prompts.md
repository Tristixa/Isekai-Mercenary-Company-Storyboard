No image model was invoked. `sources/art.js` is the complete drawing source; `sources/generated/` contains its untouched 2× PNG outputs before packaging. `sources/reference/approved-kit.js` preserves the original kit implementation for comparison. Reference copies and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources/provenance.json`.

Separate outer bezel, crimson outer enemy track, deep blue inner party track, gold divider and dark inner line. Each track has exactly sixty white minute ticks, with every fifth longer. Keep its shared canvas for rotation. No emblems or letters.
